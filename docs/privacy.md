# Privacy and public data minimization

The private study used a small public social-media sample, but public availability does not grant unrestricted dataset redistribution. This repository contains no original user nicknames, account/profile IDs, avatar links, original note/comment IDs, login data or bulk83-comment export.

Examples are clearly labeled Example data and use paraphrases plus unrelated aliases. They cannot be used as verbatim user quotes or the complete dataset. Screenshots are actual report crops limited to aggregate scope and limitations; they are not doctored research outcomes. The full HTML/MD/PDF report with identifiable source links remains private. Official product source URLs may remain for attribution.

Only aggregate evaluation numbers and hashes of locally inspected evidence are published. Counts are not independent-user frequencies. Any future data collection or public disclosure needs its own authorization, rights and platform-policy review.
