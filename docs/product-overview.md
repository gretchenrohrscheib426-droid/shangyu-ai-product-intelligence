# Product overview

**Shangyu — Multi-Agent AI Product Sentiment & User Feedback Intelligence Platform**

A multi-agent AI research platform for analyzing real-world user feedback, public sentiment, external media, product facts, and evidence-backed product opportunities.

Primary users for this portfolio: AI product managers, product operations researchers and LLM application engineers. The workflow supports VOC triage, research questions, feature discovery, content-operation hypotheses and risk review. Continuous monitoring, causal product impact and representative sentiment measurement are not validated capabilities.

The local case is Doubao AI Assistant user experience, reputation and product opportunities. The release contains documentation, selected new source references and offline checks; the complete running platform stays private because third-party redistribution rights are unresolved.
