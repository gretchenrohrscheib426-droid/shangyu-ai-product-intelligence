# Legal and licensing scope

The repository uses PORTFOLIO_ONLY mode. The original [upstream](https://github.com/JxKim/sentiment_analysis_platform) lacks a root license in the audited archive. MediaCrawler has its own non-commercial learning restrictions. The complete original app, modified upstream files, crawler, UI/logo and vendor/font/model files are excluded.

The [final license audit](../FINAL_LICENSE_AUDIT.md) defines the exact included categories and permissions. Newly authored additions, documents and minimized assets are published as a source-visible portfolio under default copyright. No blanket MIT/Apache or commercial reuse license is granted. Owner authorization to publish the portfolio does not relicense upstream material.

The package contains original local source references and executable pure contracts, not a complete app distribution. UI screenshots were omitted because asset permissions were not established. Three real report crops show only cover, aggregate scope and limitations. Full reports and raw user data stay private.

The audit PASS applies to the reduced allowlist only; full-source redistribution remains blocked. Future additions require their own origin, privacy and license review. Keep the [notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) and [contribution attribution](../NOTICE.md).
